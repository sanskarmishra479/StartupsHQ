# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues **privately** through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue or pull request.

Include what you found, where, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and will keep you informed while we investigate.

## Scope

- The code in this repository.
- The deployed startupsHQ sites, once live.

Out of scope: denial-of-service and volumetric testing, social engineering, physical attacks, and vulnerabilities in third-party services we don't control.

## Good faith

We won't pursue action against research that is done in good faith, avoids privacy violations and data destruction, doesn't degrade the service for others, and gives us reasonable time to fix an issue before any disclosure.
